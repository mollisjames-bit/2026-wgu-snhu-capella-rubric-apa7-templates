## Top 4 WGU C207 Evaluator Kickback Triggers
1. **Missing Explicit Hypotheses:** State both the Null Hypothesis (H0) and Alternative Hypothesis (H1) verbatim under your methodology heading.
2. **Unjustified Statistical Test:** Explain why you selected Linear Regression, ANOVA, t-test, or Chi-Square based on your independent and dependent variable types.
3. **Uninterpreted Excel Output:** State alpha = 0.05, cite the exact p-value and R-squared (or F-stat), explicitly reject or fail to reject H0, and translate the numbers into a management decision.
4. **Missing Dataset Limitations:** Include at least two concrete limitations before your final strategic recommendations.
